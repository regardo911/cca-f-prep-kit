SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-03-01

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Vantage Loom Ltd ("Vantage") and Peridot Analytics GmbH ("Peridot").

1. PURPOSE

Peridot Analytics GmbH grants Vantage Loom Ltd a non-exclusive licence to the Peridot dashboard for up to 250 named seats.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Fees are EUR 4,200 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears, net 30 from invoice date. Seat overages bill at EUR 22 per seat per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

Either party may terminate for convenience on 60 days written notice. Termination for material breach requires 30 days to cure.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
