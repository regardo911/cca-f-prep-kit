DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-01-30

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Tessellate Studios ("Tessellate") and Ninebark Interactive ("Ninebark").

1. PURPOSE

Tessellate Studios develops and delivers a co-op mode for Ninebark Interactive's title under a milestone schedule.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

USD 640,000 total across six milestones: 15, 15, 20, 20, 20 and 10 percent, each net 30 from acceptance.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The agreement ends on final milestone acceptance, or on 30 days notice for material breach uncured.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the Province of British Columbia and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
