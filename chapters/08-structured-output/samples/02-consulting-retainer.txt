CONSULTING RETAINER

Effective date: 2026-01-15

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Harbourline Consulting LLC ("Harbourline") and Ashgrove Freight Inc ("Ashgrove").

1. PURPOSE

Harbourline Consulting LLC provides logistics network modelling services to Ashgrove Freight Inc under statements of work.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

USD 18,000 per month retainer covering 80 hours. Additional hours at USD 275. Invoices net 15.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

This retainer runs 12 months and renews automatically for successive 12 month terms unless either party gives 90 days notice.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the State of Delaware and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
