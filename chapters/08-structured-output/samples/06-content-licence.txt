CONTENT LICENCE

Effective date: 2025-09-05

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Fernpath Publishing ("Fernpath") and Kestrel Media Group ("Kestrel").

1. PURPOSE

Fernpath Publishing licenses its back catalogue of 1,400 articles to Kestrel Media Group for syndication in North America.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Royalty of USD 0.04 per article view, reconciled quarterly, minimum guarantee USD 60,000 per year.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The licence runs 3 years. Either party may terminate on 120 days notice after the first anniversary.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the State of New York and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
