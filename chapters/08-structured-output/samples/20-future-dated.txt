TURBINE OVERHAUL AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2036-09-01

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Whitfell Energy ("Whitfell") and Grindley Turbines Ltd ("Grindley").

1. PURPOSE

Grindley Turbines Ltd will overhaul six turbines at the Whitfell Energy coastal site.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

GBP 2,400,000 fixed price, 25 percent on mobilisation and the balance on completion of each unit. Net 30.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The agreement runs 18 months from the effective date, extendable once by 6 months on mutual agreement.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
