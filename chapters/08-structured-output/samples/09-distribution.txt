DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2025-12-01

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Corvid Instruments ("Corvid") and Pacific Rim Scientific Pte Ltd ("Pacific").

1. PURPOSE

Pacific Rim Scientific Pte Ltd is appointed non-exclusive distributor of Corvid Instruments products in Singapore and Malaysia.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Distributor buys at 42 percent off list. Payment in USD, net 60 from shipment. Annual minimum purchase USD 750,000.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

Initial term of 2 years, renewing annually. Either party may terminate on 180 days notice.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
