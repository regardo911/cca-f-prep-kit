WAREHOUSE LEASE

Effective date: 2025-11-01

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Calder Property Trust ("Calder") and Nine Points Distribution Co ("Nine").

1. PURPOSE

Calder Property Trust leases Unit 7, Merrow Industrial Estate, 42,000 sq ft, to Nine Points Distribution Co.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Rent is GBP 31,500 per quarter payable in advance on the usual quarter days. Service charge reconciled annually.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The term is 5 years with a tenant break at the end of year 3 on 6 months notice.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
