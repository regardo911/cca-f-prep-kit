PACKAGING SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2025-10-12

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Sunhollow Foods SA ("Sunhollow") and Braeburn Packaging NV ("Braeburn").

1. PURPOSE

Braeburn Packaging NV supplies moulded fibre trays to Sunhollow Foods SA across three plants.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Unit price EUR 0.31 for the Belgian plant and CHF 0.34 for the Swiss plant. Payment net 30 in the invoiced currency.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The agreement runs 4 years with an annual price review each January.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of Belgium and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
