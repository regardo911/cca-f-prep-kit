REVENUE SHARE AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-06-01

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Larkfield Music Co ("Larkfield") and Osier Streaming Inc ("Osier").

1. PURPOSE

Larkfield Music Co makes its catalogue available on the Osier streaming service.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Osier remits 54 percent of net subscription revenue attributable to Larkfield streams, paid quarterly within 60 days of quarter end.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The initial term is 2 years, then month to month until either party gives 60 days notice.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the State of Tennessee and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
