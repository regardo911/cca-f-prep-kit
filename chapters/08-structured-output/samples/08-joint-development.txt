JOINT DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-01-08

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Ilmara Biosciences ("Ilmara") and Northgate University ("Northgate").

1. PURPOSE

Ilmara Biosciences and Northgate University jointly develop an assay platform under a shared project plan.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Ilmara funds USD 1,200,000 across eight quarterly payments of USD 150,000 each, net 30.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The collaboration runs for 24 months. Either party may withdraw on 6 months notice after month 12.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
