MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-06-15

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Redwater Utilities ("Redwater") and Hollis Field Services ("Hollis").

1. PURPOSE

Hollis Field Services performs scheduled and emergency maintenance on Redwater Utilities pumping stations.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Fixed fee CAD 46,000 per month for scheduled work. Emergency callouts at CAD 1,850 per visit plus parts. Net 30.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The agreement runs 36 months. Redwater may terminate for convenience on 90 days notice with a break fee of one month's fixed fee.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the Province of Ontario and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
