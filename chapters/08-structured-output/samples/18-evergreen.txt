CLAIMS HANDLING AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2025-08-19

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Pellwood Insurance ("Pellwood") and Cardamom Claims Ltd ("Cardamom").

1. PURPOSE

Cardamom Claims Ltd handles first notification of loss and initial triage for Pellwood Insurance motor policies.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

GBP 14.50 per claim handled, invoiced monthly, net 21. Volume rebate above 5,000 claims per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The agreement continues indefinitely until terminated by either party on 12 months written notice.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
