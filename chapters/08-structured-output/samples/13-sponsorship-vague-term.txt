SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-07-01

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Halloway Sports Trust ("Halloway") and Vireo Drinks Ltd ("Vireo").

1. PURPOSE

Vireo Drinks Ltd becomes official hydration partner of the Halloway Sports Trust regional league.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

GBP 240,000 per season, half on signature and half on the opening fixture.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The sponsorship ends when the parties agree it has run its course.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of Scotland and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
