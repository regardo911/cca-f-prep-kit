PILOT SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-05-30

PARTIES

This agreement is made between IQ ("IQ") and Bramblewood Analytics Corporation ("Bramblewood").

1. PURPOSE

IQ engages Bramblewood Analytics Corporation to run a 10 week pilot of its forecasting service.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Pilot fee USD 24,000, invoiced in two instalments, net 30.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The pilot runs 10 weeks and converts to an annual subscription unless either party gives 14 days notice before the end.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the State of Washington and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
