EQUIPMENT SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Effective date: 2026-04-10

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Bellwether Machining ("Bellwether") and Ostrand Tooling AB ("Ostrand").

1. PURPOSE

Ostrand Tooling AB supplies four CNC lathes, model OT-940, with installation and operator training.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

Total price SEK 3,850,000. 30 percent on order, 60 percent on delivery, 10 percent on acceptance. All amounts net 45.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

Either party may terminate for insolvency immediately. Otherwise the agreement runs until final acceptance plus a 24 month warranty period.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of Sweden and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
