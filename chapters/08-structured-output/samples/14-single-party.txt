BOARD RESOLUTION AND UNDERTAKING

Effective date: 2026-04-22

PARTIES

This instrument is given by Ridgeway Holdings ("Ridgeway").

1. PURPOSE

Ridgeway Holdings resolves to guarantee the obligations of its subsidiaries under their trade finance facilities.

2. PAYMENT TERMS

No consideration is payable. The guarantee is given for corporate benefit.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

The undertaking remains in force for 7 years from the date of this resolution.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of Jersey and the parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of its courts.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter
and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the parties on the effective date above.
